How we work

One brief. One team. One bill.

Most companies juggle an agency, a developer, an accountant and an investor who never talk to each other. At Redbarn they sit at the same table.

  1. 01

    Talk

    Tell us where you are and where you want to go. One conversation, no deck needed.

  2. 02

    Plan

    We scope the work across every discipline it touches and come back with a clear plan, timeline and quote.

  3. 03

    Deliver

    Specialists do the work. You keep one point of contact from the first call to the final handover.

  4. 04

    Grow

    Stay on with us for ongoing design, IT and accounts, or for capital when you are ready to scale.
