Privacy policy

Last updated 2 November 2026

Who we are

Redbarn Ventures ("Redbarn", "we", "us") provides design, IT, investment and accounting services and operates across Amsterdam, Ahmedabad, London and Oslo. This policy explains what personal data we collect through this website, why, and the rights you have over it. Questions go to admin@redbarn.ventures.

What we collect

  • Contact form: your name, email address, company or project, the services you select and the message you write.
  • Email: anything you send to our address, including attachments.
  • Technical data: our hosting provider (Cloudflare) processes IP addresses and request logs to serve and secure the site. We do not run analytics or advertising trackers on this site.

How the contact form works

Messages sent through the form are delivered to our inbox by FormSubmit, an email forwarding service. FormSubmit processes the content of your message only to deliver it to us.

Why we use it

Our legal bases under the GDPR and UK GDPR are your consent (when you contact us), our legitimate interests in responding to enquiries and running our business, and, where we go on to work together, performance of a contract.

  • To reply to your enquiry and, if appropriate, discuss working together.
  • To evaluate proposals and pitches you send us.
  • To keep the website secure and available.

How long we keep it

Enquiries and correspondence are kept for as long as needed to deal with them and for up to three years afterwards, unless a longer period is required by law or we enter into a business relationship.

Who we share it with

We do not sell personal data. We share it only with service providers who help us operate, such as email, form and hosting providers, and only as needed to provide those services. Where data leaves the EEA or UK, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

Your rights

To exercise any of these, email admin@redbarn.ventures. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Ask us to correct or delete it.
  • Object to or restrict how we use it.
  • Receive a copy in a portable format.
  • Withdraw consent at any time, where consent is the basis.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version is always at redbarn.ventures/privacy.
